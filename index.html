Dr. Wenchao Yue

Science has been my way of seeking truth and of finding my place all around the world. I received my Ph.D. in Electronic Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) (2026), including a period as a visiting Ph.D. researcher at École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL). Before that, I earned an M.Sc. with Distinction in Mechanical Engineering from the National University of Singapore (NUS) (2021), spent a fully sponsored exchange year at Institut Polytechnique des Sciences Avancées (IPSA), Paris, and received my B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA) (2020).

Now I work as a robotics engineer on embodied AI and manipulation. I'm currently a research intern in the Foundation Model Team at Sharpa Robotics, building tactile foundation models for contact-rich dexterous manipulation, and was previously an algorithm intern in the Embodied Robotic Manipulation Team at MindOn Robotics, post-training compliant manipulation policies. I firmly believe that general-purpose robotics and AI are what will truly transform people's lives.

Research Philosophy

I aspire to train physical foundation models that generalize and adapt, much as I have trained myself through wide-ranging scientific exploration. My doctoral research spanned the fabrication and deployment of robotic tactile sensors and their integration with robot learning and control — from Bayesian active palpation to incision planning — bringing dexterous manipulation and task-level autonomy to surgical robots and clinical devices. In industry I now carry this into embodied AI: integrating the tactile modality into robot foundation models across pre-, mid- and post-training, and post-training vision–language–action policies with force/tactile injection for contact-rich manipulation. My research interests include the following areas and sub-areas; numbers in parentheses refer to the publication list below.

Machine learning & embodied AI: tactile foundation models and force/tactile-VLA post-training, Bayesian active palpation and incision recommendation (10, 23), learning-based ultrasound perception for surgical guidance (27), deep reinforcement learning for tumor palpation (7), sim-to-real policy transfer (8).

Tactile sensing: OCT-based tactile sensors for intraluminal palpation (14, 17), piezoelectric tactile sensors (1, 2), vision-based tactile sensors (22), flexible and stretchable strain sensors with embodied inflation perception (15, 18), origami-inspired force sensing (4), force-sensing surgical instruments (6), haptic feedback in robot-assisted surgery (11).

Robotics: robot-assisted tracheostomy (12, 13), augmented-reality surgical guidance (12), teleoperation and micromanipulation (20, 21), continuum, capsule and endoluminal robots for biopsy (3, 6, 25, 26), magnetic and reconfigurable robots (5, 19), soft actuators — thermo-pneumatic pouch motors, balloon actuators and bistable magnetoelastic origami (9, 16, 24, 26).
